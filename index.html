The Junas method

Understanding is what makes the shapes stick.

You'll memorize shapes, and you should. You just shouldn't memorize them blind. These are the three things most beginner content skips, and the three things Junas builds first.

1

Know the fretboard

Learn the notes and where they actually sit on the neck, so you stop guessing and start knowing.

2

Build real technique

Proper strumming and plucking with all your fingers, not just one. This is where most bad habits start, so it gets fixed early.

3

Play chords that sound good

Move past basic open shapes into the fuller voicings that actually sound good, always securing the bass note first.

Common questions.

I know zero about music. Is this for me?

Yes. It starts from the very beginning: how to hold the guitar, tune it, and strum. No prior experience needed.

Acoustic or electric?

Acoustic. That's what Junas plays, teaches, and has built his method around. Junas is a multi-instrumentalist, so as the Learning Center grows, other instruments and styles will be added. Right now, every lesson is built for acoustic guitar.

How are the 1:1 calls structured?

Each call is a full 60 minutes, live, one-on-one with Junas over video. Junas watches you play, finds the root of the problem, and gives you the specific fix on the spot. The more precise your questions are, the more you walk away with. Book as many or as few calls as you need.

You'll still get value. Knowing the shapes is a head start, not a reason to skip. The tracks show you where those shapes come from, so the ones you already know finally make sense, and the next ones take half the time.
